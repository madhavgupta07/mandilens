## What MandiLens is

MandiLens is an open-source, AI-assisted market intelligence app for agricultural trading. It turns publicly available mandi prices (AGMARKNET via data.gov.in) into a practical decision tool: compare markets by estimated net proceeds after your expenses, explore commodities and markets, discover buy/sell listings, and ask a grounded AI Copilot that shows its work.

## The problem it solves

Small farmers and traders often have to decide which mandi to sell in with incomplete information. A higher quoted price can be wiped out by transport, loading, commission or market charges. Public price data exists, but converting it to "net proceeds for this quantity and these costs" is fiddly. MandiLens makes that explicit and traceable.

## Key features

- **Compare workbench** — Compare candidate markets by estimated net proceeds (gross − total estimated expenses). Ranks by net proceeds (not headline price), shows the gap to the best option, warnings and missing assumptions. Supports manual prices and auto-comparison by commodity + location.
- **Deterministic calculation engine** — Unit conversions (kg/quintal/tonne), expense computation (flat or % commission), currency in INR. Pure functions; identical inputs give identical outputs. 15+ unit tests cover it.
- **Grounded AI Copilot** — Deterministic-first: rule-based NLU calls tools (prices, compare, history, listings, freshness). Answers include a provenance banner ("not generated by an AI model"), a tool trace, grounded/missingData flags, and a disclaimer. Never invents prices or numbers.
- **Public explorers** — Browse commodities, markets, price history and freshness. Server components with graceful fallbacks when the DB is unavailable.
- **Listings marketplace** — Buy/sell listings with matching (opposite direction), inquiries with threads and notifications.
- **Auth & dashboards** — First-party auth (bcrypt + HS256 httpOnly cookie), alerts, watchlists, saved comparisons.
- **Admin console** — Sync sources, run AGMARKNET ingestion on demand, upload/preview/commit CSV/JSON (two-phase), moderate reported listings, manage users, view audit log.
- **Data integrity** — Every price keeps source + reported date; demo data badged and separated; dedupe by composite key; provenance model; caps on imports.

## How it demonstrates open-source AI

- Defaults to `AI_PROVIDER=deterministic` — runs without a model key and still useful.
- Model-agnostic: supports Ollama (local) and OpenAI-compatible providers (including open-weight Gemma). No hard dependency on a single vendor.
- Grounding is enforced: the assistant must call tools and must not override the deterministic engine. Reliability tests verify this.
- Transparent: tool traces, `grounded` and `missingData` flags, explicit notes about non-model authorship.

## What’s tested

- Unit: units, dates/freshness, opportunity engine, NLU, importer mapping/dedupe.
- Integration: ingestion idempotency + provenance + market/commodity creation; listings create/list/search/matches; inquiries create/list/reply.
- Reliability: deterministic assistant grounding, help/clarification paths, tool provenance, "never invents data".

All 73 tests pass (`pnpm test`).

## Technical highlights

- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4.
- MongoDB with 15 models and appropriate indexes (text, compound, unique `dedupeKey`).
- Decimal.js everywhere for money (avoids floating point).
- AGMARKNET ingestion adapter with retry-safe dedupe and provenance recording.
- lucide-react v1.57.0 compatible (brand icons removed; uses Code etc.).
